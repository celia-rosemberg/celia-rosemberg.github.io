Proyecto Alfabetización Familiar

About the project

Proyecto Alfabetización Familiar is an academic initiative coordinated by Celia Rosemberg and Diego Fernández Meijide. It aims to support families in reading, conversation, vocabulary and play activities shared with five-year-old children.

Teachers share the activities in the usual WhatsApp groups. Families may respond voluntarily and privately to the project's number with text messages, voice notes or photographs of drawings and activities.

Participation

  • Participation is voluntary.
  • Not responding, or choosing to stop participating, does not affect the relationship with the preschool, the classroom or the teachers.
  • There is no need to send faces, identity documents, addresses or any additional information.
  • Materials involving children must be sent with the authorization of a responsible adult.

This page does not replace the informed consent of the study.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026.

Information received

When a family writes to the project's WhatsApp number, the system may receive:

  • WhatsApp number or identifier.
  • Profile name, if WhatsApp provides it.
  • Date, time and message identifier.
  • Internal code for the family, classroom, preschool or activity.
  • Texts, voice notes, photographs of drawings and other images sent voluntarily.
  • Records needed to confirm receipt and operate the system.

Beyond that:

  • The system does not access the preschool's groups.
  • It does not access the contacts list.
  • It does not access other conversations.
  • It only receives messages sent directly to the project's number.

Purposes

  • Receive and organize the responses.
  • Associate them with the corresponding activity.
  • Confirm their receipt.
  • Measure participation.
  • Resolve operational errors.
  • Carry out academic analysis using dissociated data.

Excluded uses

  • The data is not sold.
  • It is not used for advertising.
  • Identifiable materials are not published without additional authorization.

Providers

The system uses or will use:

  • Meta and the WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Render and PostgreSQL.
  • Backblaze B2.

These providers may process information outside Argentina. These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and use the same web typeface as the rest of the site; those services may receive technical connection data under their own policies. None of these providers sponsors the project.

Security

The system is designed to use private storage, restricted access and separation between identifiers and analysis data.

Retention

Identifiable original materials will be kept for a maximum of 24 months after the formal end of fieldwork. They will then be deleted, and only effectively dissociated data may be kept, that is, data that does not allow a person or family to be reasonably identified.

Rights

Families may at any time:

  • Ask what identifiable information is kept.
  • Request a correction.
  • Withdraw their consent.
  • Request the deletion of the materials.

To request deletion, see the data deletion instructions. For any other question: celiarosemberg@gmail.com.
