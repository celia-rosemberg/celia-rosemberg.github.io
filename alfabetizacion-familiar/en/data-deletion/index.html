Data deletion

A family may request the deletion of the identifiable messages and materials sent to the WhatsApp number of Proyecto Alfabetización Familiar.

How to request it

  1. Send an email to celiarosemberg@gmail.com.
  2. Use this subject line: Data deletion — Proyecto Alfabetización Familiar.
  3. Indicate the WhatsApp number or identifier used.
  4. Indicate, if known, the family, classroom or preschool code.
  5. State whether you are requesting the deletion of all the information or only of certain files.

Do not send photographs of identity documents.

Do not send passwords or verification codes.

What happens next

  • The team may carry out a reasonable check to prevent someone from deleting another person's data.
  • The identifiable files and links under the project's control will be deleted.
  • Data that has already been irreversibly dissociated may not be individually locatable.
  • The request does not affect the relationship with the preschool, the classroom or the teachers.
